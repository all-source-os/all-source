## Design Context

### Users

Builders and platform teams shipping production or serious pre-production AI
agents and event-driven systems. They need to record durable history, query and
replay state, preserve provenance, and give agents memory that survives process
and session boundaries.

### Brand Personality

Evidence-led, technical, dependable. AllSource should feel like trustworthy
infrastructure: precise enough for incident analysis, calm enough for long-form
documentation, and direct about product boundaries and limits.

### Aesthetic Direction

Public marketing background is fixed to the supplied logo PNG's `#0E1A2A`
corner color in both theme states. Raised surfaces use mid-blue; actions and
trace signals use ice and signal blue. Blue-tinted paper is for readable
technical documents, not public page backgrounds. Ordered event rails and real
system traces are the signature device. Avoid purple decoration, generic neon
AI gradients, glass panels, and ornamental technical imagery.

Use supplied AllSource logo unchanged: no recoloring, cropping, rotation, or
effects. Keep minimum 32 px size and clear space equal to half logo width.

### Design Principles

1. Make durable history and provenance visible through real product evidence.
2. Use semantic tokens from `docs/brand/BRAND_GUIDE.md`; never invent page-local
   palette values.
3. Keep public fields on the logo backdrop, technical code surfaces source-ink,
   and status colors strictly semantic.
4. Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA, responsive 320 px layouts, and readable line lengths.
5. Preserve one clear primary action per page and strip decorative AI slop.
