01 / The workflow

From a question
to a conversation.

Start with a hypothesis. Keep the evidence beside each prospect, review every draft, and let the answers shape what you learn next.

01 / FIND

Choose the right people

Define the audience, check public evidence, and review existing contact history before preparing a list.

02 / PREPARE

Ask a useful question

Adapt research-first templates to each person. Separate what you know from what you want to find out.

03 / LEARN

Bring the human in

Approve the exact recipient and message before sending. Capture replies and hand the conversation to its owner.

02 / Start here
