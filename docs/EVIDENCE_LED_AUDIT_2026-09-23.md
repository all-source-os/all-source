## Decision

Keep the existing contract and fixed gate. Investigate the named assumption before expanding acquisition or product scope. This audit does not establish that a kill or promotion condition has fired; current trusted outcome totals remain unverified in this pass.

## Findings and counterevidence

September demand research supports recovery and provenance pain, but native persistence and existing databases remain credible substitutes. IMPACT_PLAN records unresolved agent-first versus event-store-first positioning. Independent paid renewal evidence is not established by the reviewed records.

Strongest alternative explanation: A persistent Postgres/SQLite checkpointer or existing event log may solve the job with less infrastructure.

## Investigation

Question: Does a recovery-critical builder obtain enough operational benefit over their existing checkpointer to pay and renew without bespoke engineering?

Bounded protocol: One consented non-founder builder first compares an existing persistent checkpointer with the same real workload on AllSource: ingest, restart, recall, provenance, operator interventions and time to verified recovery. Record plan fit, retention and read/write limits before any priced commitment.

Outcome and regression evidence: Completed recovery and provenance; setup/recovery minutes; interventions; first verified payment and first renewal separately. Never use synthetic benchmark speed or repository stars as paid proof. Record abandonment, assistance and no-benefit outcomes as well as success; do not replace them with satisfaction or traffic.

Qualified access: Qualify up to five builders with a current restart/resume failure and a permitted technical-contribution or consented-introduction route. Reproducible public issue work precedes product links; no unsolicited issue pitches.

Stop and gate boundary: Preserve three qualifying paid-and-renewed customers, five qualified proof completers with no payment, or three bespoke onboarding attempts from BET.md. Reconcile positioning through an explicit decision before changing the contract.

## Evidence rules

1. Refresh original problem records with source URL, observation date, source family, exact trigger, current workaround and claim class. Collapse repeated posts from one source family. Absence of exact evidence is a finding, not permission to invent it.
2. Separate the business model, observed willingness to pay, literal purchase unit and customer work removed. Free use, ad-funded traffic, provider claims and paid tool demand are different evidence classes.
3. Name a permitted route to the first five qualified people, or record why access is unknown. This is an access test, not a new five-payment gate or permission for unsolicited messages.
4. Keep direct validation separate from search development. Record qualified exposures, source, window, exclusions and completed outcomes. Query volume, indexation and search data remain unknown where access is absent; SEO delay never extends a fired kill gate.
5. Trace each proposed change from source and current bottleneck to one bounded experiment, success measure, regression measure and stop condition. Missing links remain research. Necessary delivery repairs can proceed independently.
6. Inspect human-labelled representative failures before AI changes; compare completion and correction effort with a manual baseline. Keep synthetic evidence separate, and calibrate any judge against held-out human labels. Bounded automation needs measured error/cost and fallback.
7. Preserve BET.md, commercial pauses, refund duties, and complete Android/iOS plus dedicated app-page requirements. Apply relevant recent build failures as regression cases, without copying every lesson to every product.
