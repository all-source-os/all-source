Actions worth taking

ordered by money at stake
  1. allsource-core: measure for a week after gh#444 ships, then decide on 12 GB. $24.00/month. This run peaked at 8.91 GiB, the 11-08 run at 12.64 GiB. gh#444 now refuses a cold load memory cannot hold, so a one-off load no longer kills the process, but a 12 GB machine still fails on the 11-08 peak. Read VmHWM on PID 672 after seven days of uptime; downsize only if it stays under ~10 GiB.
  2. Remove allsource-core-rehearsal once the rehearsal is done. $1.50/month for the volume, plus whatever Fly bills for the stopped 16 GB machine's root filesystem. Its last deploy was 2026-11-09.
  3. Re-baseline the bandwidth warning. It is no longer firing, and the last 24 h used 22% of its 10 GB. Leaving it at 10 GB now gives early warning of a return to the 11-08 rate; lowering it to ~5 GB would catch a doubling.
  4. Confirm allsource-admin being suspended is intended. Its only machine is stopped, last deployed 2026-09-27. If admin.all-source.xyz should answer, the machine needs auto-start or a restart; if not, the app can go.
