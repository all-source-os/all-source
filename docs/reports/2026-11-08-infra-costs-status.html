Actions worth taking

ordered by money at stake
  1. allsource-core: keep 16 GB. It is 70% of Fly spend, but the Core process peaked at 12.64 GiB RSS (VmHWM) over 41.4 h of uptime and 8 GB failed on 2026-10-24. A 12 GB machine fails on the measured peak alone. Lowering the peak (archive replay, snapshot sizes) is the only route to a smaller size.
  2. Cut Longhand's repeated reads. Longhand PR #1850 caches the app-skin definition and queue-binding lists (29% of sampled large-response bytes). Auth-session reads (18–67 KB each, the same entity re-read about every 30 s), entitlement reads (73 KB) and workflow_template (2.6 MB) remain. Each GB saved is $0.02, so the money is small; the warning alert and the egress pool are the reasons.
  3. Re-baseline the bandwidth warning once the cache ships. It has fired for 13 days, so it no longer signals a change. Re-measure fly_edge_data_out after #1850 deploys, then set the warning above the new steady state, or keep 10 GB if the remaining reads are cut.
  4. Builder volume: $7.50/month for a stopped builder. fly-builder-sparkling-star-9149 (suspended) keeps a 50 GB volume in lhr. Removing the builder frees it; Fly creates a new one on the next remote build, which then starts cold.
  5. Unattached Core volume: $1.50/month. vol_vwjoq95l03qzy88r (10 GB) has no machine and was created 8 months ago. Confirm it holds nothing the 2026-10-13 archive recovery needs before deleting it.
  6. Route Longhand to the gateway over Flycast. Saves up to the egress line ($10–20/month at current rates) if Flycast traffic is billed as private transfer, which Fly's docs do not state. It needs a gateway service without force_https (Flycast is HTTP-only) and a private IPv6 allocated to the longhand org.
  7. Confirm the org's data-transfer pricing mode. If the org is not on granular rates, same-region private traffic (gateway → Core) is billed at the public rate.
  8. Fix the Disk Alarm workflow. 90 of 109 runs failed in 30 days. It costs nothing on a public repo, but a red alarm that always fails hides the one that matters.

What I have NOT checked

  • The Fly invoice: there is no CLI path to current-period spend. Core's shared-cpu-8x price is derived from the 256 MB and 512 MB list prices.
  • Rootfs charges for the stopped builder machine, and whether lhr volumes cost the iad $0.15/GB.
  • Egress older than two days: the alert Prometheus keeps 2 days or 512 MiB, so there is no 7-day series. Fly's managed Prometheus keeps longer but needs an org token.
  • The Egress Attribution dashboard (fly-metrics.net/d/allsource-egress), which names route and tenant per byte.
  • That fingerprint 2732b1a7938f is Longhand's tenant: inferred from co-occurring logs, not by hashing the tenant id.
  • Why hourly egress halved after 2026-11-07 22:00Z.
  • Billable Actions minutes per workflow: the chart shows run wall-clock time.
  • Whether the org is on granular data-transfer rates.
