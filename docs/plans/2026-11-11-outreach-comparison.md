# AllSource outreach comparison

Status: proposed workflows; no campaign or tracking initialized. The conversation identifies two objectives: learn whether potential users experience problems AllSource could address, and evaluate a reusable outreach workflow. Fintech technical decision-makers, audit trails, and compliance are starting hypotheses, not a finalized audience or validated needs.

## Shared brief and controls

Agree the target role, company criteria, geography, problem hypotheses, two research questions, channel, sender, and review owner before sourcing. Use public professional evidence and channels; check the existing [outreach ledger](../outreach/qualified-builder-outreach.md) for prior contact and exclusions. Keep warm introductions separate from the cold comparison. Never use scraped contact data or personal email. Exact recipient and message require founder approval before sending. One initial message; follow-up only when explicitly approved and due.

Allocate eligible, deduplicated prospects to the two workflows using a recorded random assignment, balanced where practical by role and company type. Do not send to the same person from both arms. Use equivalent problem questions, channel, sender, review criteria, and observation window. Record any differences in sourcing, personalization, or manual intervention; this compares complete workflows, not only underlying software. Small samples produce directional findings, not causal proof.

## Flow A: GojiBerry

1. **Inspect access and existing state.** Campaign and Contacts screens redirected to pricing on November 11. Dashboard displayed 10 active signals, two LinkedIn accounts, and a next launch with zero invitations and zero messages listed. Later activity was not established. The owner should review existing automation independently before any new campaign.
2. **Prepare configuration for review.** Map the shared brief to source signals or an approved list, ICP filters, message variants, and limits using [campaign guidance](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960282-how-to-create-a-campaign). Verify actual account access and costs before purchase or trial. Do not launch or initialize a campaign without approval.
3. **Review candidates and drafts.** Verify sources, fit, contact history, and assignment. [Review Mode](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/16544532-what-is-review-mode) approves leads into an automated sequence; it does not establish individual-message approval. Keep prospects out of the sequence until exact-recipient and message approval is secured, and verify any actual send controls. If that cannot be done, use a manually controlled channel or stop this arm at a reviewable draft.
4. **After explicit approval:** send via the approved channel; capture outcome and any blocks, refusals, or opt-outs. Route replies to the agreed human owner. Stop outreach to anyone who declines.

## Flow B: AllSource, informed by ritual-full

1. **Think:** use the same brief and evidence standard. Research candidates through permitted public sources; retain source links, dates, fit rationale, and uncertainty. Compare against existing contacts before assignment.
2. **Spec and plan:** describe the operator's end-to-end job: find and qualify a prospect, prepare a tailored research question, review it, record a send after approval, and route a reply to a human. Identify each intended data write and outbound action, its owner, and the check before it happens. Do not assume a CRM feature exists because it appears in a design document.
3. **Build and review:** first inspect the actual Prime tools and account permissions. Prepare a minimal, reviewable workflow using verified capabilities; mark manual steps and gaps. Test with synthetic records before handling real prospects. Verify no message or external data write occurs before its approval. If implementation is requested, apply the relevant software-delivery gates separately.
4. **After explicit approval:** record approved outreach and responses in the agreed store; hand conversations to the human owner. Review provenance and outcomes, then refine the workflow.

## Measurement and decisions

For each arm, record candidate source and count; eligible and excluded counts with reasons; approved and sent messages; replies; relevant problem evidence; conversations agreed; declines, blocks and opt-outs; tool spend; and human work involved. Keep message variants and assignment with each result. Use sent messages as the reply-rate denominator, and report counts alongside rates. Do not count warm introductions in either arm. Record observations and actual costs only after authorized activity; this document creates no tracking store.

Review whether the problem recurs in substantive responses, whether either workflow produces qualified leads without excessive rejection, and which manual steps or costs prevent completion. A 10% reply rate was mentioned in conversation as encouraging, not fixed as a success threshold. Non-response alone cannot disprove the problem.
