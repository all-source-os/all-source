## Approved duration and proposed operating rules

1. Retain the existing 14-day trial. The trial is not one of the 12 paid months.
2. Apply the agreed plan discount for the first 12 paid monthly subscription periods. Payment retries must not consume another discounted period. Confirm how the billing provider handles pauses, failed payments, credits, refunds, and plan changes before activation; do not silently substitute 12 calendar months from signup.
3. Apply this self-serve offer to monthly subscriptions only. No stacking with annual-billing savings, another coupon, credits, or a separate partner promotion. A plan switch must not restart the 12-month entitlement.
4. Verify each referral. Allow one offer per company; do not publish a reusable public coupon.
5. Preserve standard quotas and support. Exclude metered usage, dedicated infrastructure, and engineering services from the subscription discount.
6. Disclose the renewal price and send a reminder before the discount ends. Any Enterprise discount duration and renewal must be explicit in its quote.
7. Limit the initial cohort. The cohort cap, redemption window, and eligible-customer definition remain undecided; no numbers have been approved.
8. Confirm margins and support capacity before issuing discounts. The proposed rates are not evidence that each tier is profitable at those prices.

Annual prices observed on 2026-09-25 were £181.99 for Indie, £757.99 for Studio, and £2,869.99 for Scale. The public annual plan already saved roughly 20% compared with twelve standard monthly payments. Leave those plans unchanged.
